About Ask Kynvera

A simple guide for everyone

What this is

Ask Kynvera is a helper built into Kynvera. Type a question in everyday English — the same way you would ask a colleague — about your work in this app.

What you can ask

  • How many forms are waiting for your review
  • When you last took leave, and other leave details
  • Your tickets and work orders
  • Help finding a document or policy
  • How a module works, or details on your own profile

What it can do for you

It can look up your own records and explain next steps. It can also prepare a ticket draft or a leave draft.

Nothing is saved until you tap Confirm. A draft is not submitted. You still finish it on the real form if a signature or extra details are needed.

Example questions

  • How many pending forms?
  • When was my last leave?
  • Find the safety policy
  • Create a ticket: AC leak at HQ
  • Save annual leave from 1 to 5 September

Typical scenarios

  • Catching up on reviews — a supervisor checks how many forms still need a sign-off.
  • Checking your leave — you ask when you last took leave instead of opening HR menus.
  • Reporting a fault — you describe a leak or breakdown in chat and confirm a ticket draft for a supervisor to review.
  • Starting leave — you give the dates in chat, confirm a draft, then open the leave form to sign and submit.

Please note

  • Answers are for the account you are signed in with.
  • It will not invent policy numbers, addresses, or details it does not have.
  • It will not submit forms, approve requests, or close tickets for you.
  • If it is not sure, it will say so and point you to the right screen or an administrator.
